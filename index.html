Frequently Asked Questions

Is the callout and fitment really free?

Walk in at 28 St Columb Rd, New Redruth: testing and fitment are free when you buy the battery from us — there is no callout charge in the shop. If we come to you, a callout fee applies. We confirm that fee before dispatch. You pay after the engine starts.

What brands do you stock?

We are stockists of Willard, Exide, Enertec, Power Plus, Eco Plus and Raylite. As an independent Alberton battery shop we quote more than one brand on the same vehicle so you can choose on specification, warranty and price.

What warranty do I get?

Warranty is the manufacturer period printed on your invoice — commonly 12, 24, 25 or 36 months depending on brand and range. We register it at professional fitment. Fitting the wrong technology (for example a standard flooded battery in an AGM/EFB Start/Stop car) can void cover.

My car has Start-Stop. Can you help?

Absolutely. We are specialists in modern AGM and EFB batteries required for Start-Stop vehicles. We also have the diagnostic tools to correctly register the new battery with your car's computer (essential for BMW, Audi, etc.).

How quickly can you get to me?

We dispatch from New Redruth during trading hours (Mon–Fri 08:00–17:00, Sat 08:00–12:00). Typical arrival inside Alberton, Meyersdal and Alrode is 45–60 minutes. After hours, call 010 109 6211 and we will confirm whether a technician is available — we do not advertise a 24-hour average.

Do you just sell batteries?

No. We provide a full diagnostic service. In-store and mobile visits include testing your battery, alternator, and starter to find the real problem. We will not sell you a battery you do not need.

What are your operating hours?

Monday to Friday 08:00–17:00, Saturday 08:00–12:00. Closed Sunday. The shop line is answered from 07:30; the store opens to the public at 08:00.

Do you sell inverter or bike batteries?

Yes. We stock deep cycle and high-cycle batteries for inverters, plus motorcycle, jet ski and quad-bike batteries. Car, bakkie, truck and motorcycle fitted prices include VAT and include scrap exchange of your old battery.

Do I need to bring my old battery?

Yes for automotive batteries — car, bakkie, truck and motorcycle. Listed prices include scrap exchange: bring the old unit (or have it ready for the mobile technician) and we handle core disposal. A price without scrap is quoted separately if you do not have the old battery.
